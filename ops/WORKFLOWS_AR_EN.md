# Clinic Workflows | مسارات تشغيل العيادة

## 1. Reception workflow | مسار الاستقبال

1. Search before creating a patient to avoid duplicate records.
2. Verify identity using at least two identifiers.
3. Update demographics, contact details and insurance with source/date.
4. Book the appointment under the correct facility, provider and category.
5. Confirm arrival and update appointment status.
6. Collect only the information required for reception duties.
7. Hand off to nursing/clinical staff through the configured flowboard.

1. البحث عن المريض قبل إنشاء ملف جديد.
2. التحقق باستخدام معرفين على الأقل.
3. تحديث البيانات ووسائل الاتصال والتأمين مع توثيق المصدر والتاريخ.
4. حجز الموعد بالفرع والطبيب والتصنيف الصحيح.
5. تسجيل الوصول وتحديث حالة الموعد.
6. عدم فتح البيانات السريرية غير اللازمة للاستقبال.
7. تحويل الحالة للتمريض أو الطبيب عبر مسار العمل.

## 2. Nursing workflow | مسار التمريض

- Confirm the active encounter and patient identity.
- Record vital signs, allergies, current medications and visit reason.
- Flag urgent findings according to clinic policy.
- Never overwrite prior clinical facts; add a dated correction when needed.
- Mark the patient ready for the assigned provider.

## 3. Doctor workflow | مسار الطبيب

- Review problems, allergies, medication list, history and recent results.
- Open one encounter for the current visit.
- Document history, examination, assessment and plan.
- Add diagnoses and procedures using the configured coding standard.
- Create prescriptions and laboratory/procedure orders.
- Record follow-up instructions and patient education.
- Review and sign the note; AI-generated text, when later enabled, must remain a draft until approved.

## 4. Billing workflow | مسار الفوترة

- Work only from completed/signed encounters.
- Verify services, codes, payer and required claim fields.
- Record payments and adjustments with reason and reference.
- Reconcile daily receipts and unresolved balances.
- Never alter clinical documentation to resolve a billing issue.

## 5. Patient portal workflow | مسار بوابة المريض

- Verify the patient's email and identity before enabling portal access.
- Grant access only to the correct patient record.
- Share results/documents according to clinic release policy.
- Do not send sensitive clinical details in ordinary SMS or WhatsApp messages.
- Revoke or reset access when identity or email changes.

## 6. Incident workflow | مسار الحوادث

1. Preserve logs and record the time, user and affected function.
2. Restrict access only as much as necessary to contain the incident.
3. Do not delete records or logs.
4. Restore from a tested backup only in a separate recovery plan.
5. Document actions, validation and final approval.
